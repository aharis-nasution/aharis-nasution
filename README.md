## Current Product Direction

I am planning a reporting-first SaaS platform for marketplace operations. The initial scope focuses on secure authentication, workspace access, store and product data, sales dashboards, product analysis, and report imports. Additional integrations will be prioritized after the core workflow is validated.

## Engineering Principles

- Start with a real business problem and a clearly defined user need.
- Prioritize security, privacy, maintainability, and useful documentation.
- Deliver iteratively and measure whether the solution works.
- Protect client data, credentials, and proprietary information.
- Clearly distinguish concepts, prototypes, and production-ready work.
